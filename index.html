Exposed arc is the compass range the mooring has no shelter from. It shades the compass dial, outlines gusts on the forecast timeline, and draws a wedge on the map. Default 20°–120° is east through northeast, the open-water direction for Lynn Harbor. Obs station is the NWS station for land wind — KBOS (Logan) is nearest, KBVY (Beverly) the northern alternative. Buoys and marine zones are set in scripts/collect.mjs. On iPad: Auto-Lock → Never.
